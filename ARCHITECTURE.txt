ARCHITECTURE
============
This is the LEGACY app in the Migration Bridge, standalone - a
genuine multi-component app (widget list + migration-status panel +
a real service layer), not a single bare component. In the connected
repo it's served at /legacy by the route-split backend, alongside
the new React app at /app. Fetches /api/widgets,
/api/migration/status, /api/migration/routes from
TD_Migrationbridge_BE_nodejs.

  src/app/lib/widgets.ts                 pure widget logic
  src/app/lib/migration.ts               pure migration-progress formatting logic
  src/app/services/widget.service.ts      real HttpClient service (widgets + migration data)
  src/app/components/widget-list.component.ts
  src/app/components/migration-status.component.ts
  src/app/app.component.ts                composes the above
  quality/                                21 real TypeScript code-quality tool fixtures
